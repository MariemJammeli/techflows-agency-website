Formations FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the lessons conducted live or pre-recorded?

All sessions are conducted live online so you can ask questions and build together in real time. Full HD recordings and workflow templates are uploaded after each session with lifetime access.

Do I need to know how to code to join?

No coding experience is required for our n8n and Business Automation formations. For the Advanced AI Agents formation, basic understanding of logic or JSON is helpful, but we explain all concepts step by step.

Can I get private 1-on-1 training for myself or my corporate team?

Yes! We provide tailored private cohorts and corporate packages customized to your specific company software and business workflows. Contact us for custom scheduling.

How do I enroll or ask for pricing and the detailed schedule?

Click the "Contact Me for More Infos" button on any program to chat directly with Mariem Jammeli via WhatsApp or send us an inquiry form. We'll send the full schedule, upcoming start dates, and payment options (D17, Flouci, Bank transfer, or International card).
